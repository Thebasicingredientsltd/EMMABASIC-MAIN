Upload stockists (Excel)

Add lots of shops at once from a spreadsheet. Each shop is put on the map on the Find us page using its postcode.

  1. Your sheet needs a heading row. Only Name is required, but add a Postcode so the shop can go on the map. Address, Town, Region, Website and Phone are optional. A Highlight column (yes, true or 1) makes that shop stand out on the map. Leave it blank to keep the shop as it is.
  2. Choose the file and press Check my file. Nothing changes on the website yet — you'll see a preview of what will be added first.
